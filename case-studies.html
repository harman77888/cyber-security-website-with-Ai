Operational security stories.

The following examples are fictional case studies created to demonstrate how CyberShield could structure security outcomes. Metrics are illustrative.

Threat/problem: repeated suspicious authentication patterns across customer-facing services.

CyberShield solution: centralized monitoring, threat context and incident ownership.

Implementation: identity signals mapped into the SOC workflow with sample response actions.

Illustrative result: 42% faster triage in the fictional scenario.
